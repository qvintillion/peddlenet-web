Wait for “dropped at the outpost” before you walk away. That confirmation fires only once the post has actually stored the clue. No confirmation means you have seeded an empty post — and it will look completely normal.

Every morning, per post

  • Outpost is back in its zone room after the reboot
  • App foregrounded, in its room, on mains (screen off fine, backgrounded is not)
  • Re-drop the clue — yesterday’s has hit its 24 h TTL
  • Wait for the “dropped at the outpost” confirmation
  • Second phone joins the hunt room, walks up, collects — or the post is dead
  • Card still up, bead tin topped up

“It’s not working”

  1. Are they in the hunt room? Check the room ID on their screen against the card.
  2. Standing still? 30 seconds, within a few metres. Walking past is not enough.
  3. Outpost foregrounded and in its room? Its own screen looks healthy either way — that is the trap. Check another phone can see it.
  4. Pill flickering every ~90 s? #536. Cosmetic. Collection still works. Leave it.
  5. Slow, or “Unreachable” while standing right there? Stale MAC. Wait 5 minutes. If it persists, restart the app on the phone running longest — restarting the wrong end does nothing.
  6. Still dead? Hand them the bead and the word. The game continues.
Beads are the game. The mesh is how it feels like magic when it works. Never promise the app.
